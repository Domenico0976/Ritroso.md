## Purpose

uptapix.bid exists because independent creators, AI tool builders, and small startups have no affordable, low-friction way to buy public visibility outside of social media algorithms or expensive ad platforms. Existing solutions — Product Hunt launches, Twitter/X threads, Substack sponsorships — are either one-off events, require audience building first, or cost hundreds of euros per placement. uptapix.bid solves this by offering two independent, always-on visibility channels: a paid changelog hub where builders publish updates and earn editorial exposure, and a pixel canvas where anyone can buy permanent or temporary visual space. The key differentiator is that both channels operate independently — a user can engage with one, both, or neither, with no forced cross-dependency.

## Target user

- Primary: **Solo builder / indie maker** — someone shipping an AI tool, SaaS, or side project who needs distribution without building an audience first. They publish changelogs to get seen, and optionally buy canvas space for brand presence.
- Secondary: **Small creative brand or community** — a studio, newsletter, or Discord community that wants a permanent visual presence on the canvas as a low-cost branding exercise. They may not use the changelog hub at all.

## Core promise

A builder can publish their first changelog entry and see it live in under 10 minutes for €19, with zero audience required.

## Success metrics

- P1 success: at least 10 paying users across both channels within 60 days of launch, with at least 3 recurring purchasers.
- P2 success: 50+ active paying users and a 20%+ repeat purchase rate within 6 months.
- Failure signal: fewer than 3 paying users in the first 30 days after launch, indicating the two-channel value proposition is not resonating.

## Positioning

For independent makers and small creative brands who need visibility without building an audience first, uptapix.bid is the dual-channel visibility platform that combines a paid changelog hub with a purchasable pixel canvas. Unlike social media or ad platforms, we offer permanent, search-indexable placements that users own outright.

## Out of scope (v1)

- Mobile application (web-only in v1)
- Subscription/recurring billing — one-time purchases only
- API access for third-party integrations
- Live multiplayer canvas interaction
- Native mobile app
- Marketplace or auction mechanics for canvas space
- Community features (comments, follows, notifications between users)
- Enterprise or agency pricing tiers
